Engagement models

Ways to work with us

Four commercial shapes, chosen to match the work rather than the other way round. Most clients combine two of them.

MODEL 01

Advisory Retainer

Ongoing strategic advisory access — ideal for management teams navigating an active growth phase, where the questions arrive faster than a project can answer them.

  • Monthly fee, defined access and cadence
  • Board and leadership support
  • Rolling term, reviewed quarterly
MODEL 02

Project-Based Build

Defined-scope technology or transformation build, delivered start to finish against a fixed plan with clear deliverables and acceptance.

  • Fixed scope, timeline and price
  • Named delivery team
  • Optional support and improvement tail
MODEL 03

Managed Service (BPO)

Ongoing outsourced or co-sourced operations across finance, HR, marketing, sales or admin — run against agreed service levels.

  • Monthly service fee by function and volume
  • Fully managed or co-sourced
  • Scales up and down with the business
MODEL 04

Capital Raise Engagement

Retainer plus success fee tied to a completed raise — aligned incentives from kickoff to close.

  • Retainer funds readiness and materials
  • Success fee on completion
  • Exclusive mandate for a defined period

Combining models

The common combination

A diagnostic under an advisory retainer, a project-based build to deliver the roadmap, a managed service to run what it produces, and a raise mandate when the numbers are ready to be shown.

Each step is a separate decision with its own commercial. Nothing is bundled to force the next one.
