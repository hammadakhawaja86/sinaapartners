Pillar 02

Managed Operations (BPO)

Run the back office so you can focus on growth. Fully managed or co-sourced operations across the functions that keep a business running.

Functions we run

Five functions, one accountable service.

Finance

Accounting, reporting, FP&A — a clean monthly close and numbers a board can rely on.

HR

Recruiting, payroll and people operations, run to process rather than to whoever has time.

Marketing

Content, campaigns and brand — consistent output without a standing in-house team.

Sales

Pipeline, outreach and deal support that keeps the top of the funnel moving.

Admin

Scheduling, data and document administration — the work that quietly absorbs senior time.

Or a blend

Most clients start with one function and add others as the model proves out. Co-sourcing keeps your team in control of what matters.

Two models

Fully managed, or co-sourced.

The choice is about control, not cost. We will tell you which one your situation calls for.

Fully managed

We own the function end to end against agreed service levels. Best where the function is a distraction rather than a differentiator.

Co-sourced

Your team keeps ownership and judgement; we supply capacity, process and tooling underneath. Best where domain knowledge has to stay in-house.

Why it matters at the raise

Clean operations are diligence evidence.

A reliable monthly close is worth more in a data room than any slide about operational excellence.

Running the back office properly is not only a cost story. It produces the reporting discipline, the audit trail and the management information that a capital process depends on — which is why so many of our raise mandates start from an operations engagement.
