Growth Readiness Scan

Which pillar should you start with?

Six questions about your operating model, your data, your delivery capacity and your funding position. Two minutes, no email required, answer stays in your browser.

How to read it

An honest starting point, not a sales qualifier.

The scan weighs whether your constraint sits upstream (you do not yet know what is wrong), in the middle (you know, but cannot deliver it), or downstream (you are performing and need to fund the next stage).

Nothing is submitted anywhere. If the read-out is useful, bring it to the intro call and we will pressure-test it.

How well do you understand what is driving current performance?
How reliable is your management information?
Does your technology estate support the plan?
Do you have the delivery capacity to execute?
How much senior time goes into back-office operations?
Where are you on funding the next stage?
