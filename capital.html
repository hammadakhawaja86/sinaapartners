Pillar 03

Capital

Go-to-market to raise the capital that funds growth — grounded in a diagnostic, not just a deck.

What we do

From readiness to close.

We run the raise as a process with a critical path, not as a series of introductions.

  • Investment-readiness assessment — an honest read on whether the business is fundable today, and what would make it so
  • Equity story & narrative development, built from the operating facts
  • Financial model & data room preparation that will survive diligence
  • Investor targeting & outreach strategy — the right investors, in the right order, with the right ask
  • Process management through to close, including diligence coordination

The difference

The raise is grounded in a diagnostic, not just a deck.

Capital works standalone. But it follows directly from a Strategy & Consultancy engagement — which means the equity story is built on work we did inside the business, and the data room is populated from operations we understand.

That changes what happens in diligence. When an investor pushes on a number, the answer is not a follow-up email; it is a line we already traced to its source during the diagnostic.

The process

How a mandate runs

  1. Readiness

    Assess fundability, close the gaps that would stall a process before it starts.

  2. Story & materials

    Equity narrative, model, and a data room built to answer questions before they are asked.

  3. Go to market

    Targeted outreach to a mapped investor set, managed as a live pipeline.

  4. Diligence to close

    Coordinate the process, hold the timetable, and get to signature.

Commercials

Aligned from kickoff to close.

Capital raise engagements are structured as a retainer plus a success fee tied to a completed raise. The retainer funds the work that makes a business fundable; the success fee means we are paid properly only when the round closes.
