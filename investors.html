For capital partners

An operating partner, not another adviser.

You are assessing where value can be created — before the deal, and every quarter after it. We work the same three pillars from your side of the table.

The problem we solve

The value-creation plan and the people who can execute it are rarely the same firm.

A diligence provider hands you a thesis. A systems integrator hands you a quote. Neither is accountable for whether the plan survives contact with the asset.

We are. The team that writes the operational thesis is the team that can be deployed into the portfolio company to deliver it — as a build, as a managed operations layer, or as interim capability while a permanent team is hired.

Across the hold period

Where we plug in

PRE-DEAL

Commercial & operational diligence

An operator’s read on the target: whether the operating model can carry the plan, what the technology estate really costs, and where the execution risk sits.

DAY 1–100

Value-creation planning

A diagnostic converted into a sequenced, costed and owned initiative roadmap — with a target operating model attached to it.

HOLD

Portfolio build & run

Delivery capacity for platform builds, integrations after a bolt-on, and managed back-office operations across a portfolio rather than one asset at a time.

EXIT

Exit readiness

Equity story, model and data room built from operating evidence we helped produce — so diligence confirms the story instead of testing it.

How we work with funds

Three ways in.

  • Deal-by-dealScoped diligence or a single portfolio company engagement, priced as a project.
  • Portfolio frameworkA standing agreement with pre-agreed rates and a named team, drawn down across assets as needed.
  • Operating partnerA retained relationship at fund level — involved in screening, then deployed where the thesis needs execution.
